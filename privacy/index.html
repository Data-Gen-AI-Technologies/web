Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 15, 2026

1. Introduction

DataGenAI Technologies (“we”, “us”, or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through our website, how we use it, and the choices you have regarding that information.

2. Information We Collect

When you use our contact form or otherwise reach out to us, we may collect:

  • Your name, work email address, and company name
  • The service you're interested in and any message or project details you share with us
  • Basic technical information such as browser type and pages visited, used to keep the Site running smoothly

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and follow up on service requests

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with employees or contractors who need it to respond to your inquiry, or where required by law.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information you share with us. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time by contacting us using the details below.

7. Children's Privacy

Our Site and services are intended for businesses and are not directed at individuals under the age of 18.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, contact us at sales@datagenaitech.com.
