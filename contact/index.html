How We Work

Our proven engagement model ensures a seamless experience from initial assessment to production deployment.

  1. 1Discovery Call — We understand your current environment, pain points, and migration goals. Typically 30 minutes.
  2. 2Technical Assessment — Our team analyzes your existing pipelines, mappings, and data flows to scope the migration.
  3. 3Pilot Run — We run the AI agent on a subset of your workloads so you can see real results before committing.
  4. 4Full Migration — The agent autonomously converts, validates, and self-heals across your entire environment.
  5. 5Handoff & Support — Comprehensive documentation, knowledge transfer, and ongoing optimization support.
